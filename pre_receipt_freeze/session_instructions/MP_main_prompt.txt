CENTRAL SCIENTIFIC STORY
==================================================

CHEMOSENSORY GENOTYPE
→
CHEMOSENSORY RESPONSE ARCHITECTURE

MEDICATION-RELATED RESPONSE.

S = f(G,E)

where:

G = inherited chemosensory genotype

E = environment / diet / sensory experience.

Medication response:

M = g(S,D)

where:

D = properties of the pharmaceutical chemical exposure.

Thus:

M = g(f(G,E),D)
