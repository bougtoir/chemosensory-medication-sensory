# 26 — Success criteria for a high-impact claim

All must hold; otherwise the claim level is reduced per legacy 85.
1. Frozen prediction direction supported in Family A (Holm).
2. Formal gradient (Family E) supported or at least not contradicted, when testable.
3. Negative controls behave (zero failures, `10`).
4. Results not explained by indication, polypharmacy or frequent change (within-user restriction, n_rx_ph1, S08/S11/S13/S14).
5. Sensitivity analyses directionally consistent.
6. Calibration ρ > 0 when evaluable.
7. Biological coherence with FFQ triangulation where available.
8. Claims calibrated: no intervention or causal medication-response claims.
An isolated SNP–drug association without gradient support is never presented as proof of the framework.
