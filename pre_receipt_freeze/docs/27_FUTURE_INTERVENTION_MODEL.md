# 27 — Future intervention model (not tested in ToMMo)

Hypothesis chain for future work only: chemosensory phenotype is partly plastic (S = f(G, E)); sensory conditioning or food education could shift E; if M = g(S, D), shifting S could change drug acceptability and medication patterns. Required future designs: experimental psychophysics, randomized sensory-conditioning trials with pharmacological outcomes, pre-registered. ToMMo results can at most motivate these designs; no intervention efficacy is concluded from this study.
