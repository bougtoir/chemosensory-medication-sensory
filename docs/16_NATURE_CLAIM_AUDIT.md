# 16 — Nature-Level Claim Audit

Assessment of the five increasingly strong claims against Stage 1 evidence.

| Level | Claim | Verdict | Basis |
|-------|-------|---------|-------|
| 1 | Taste receptor variants influence pharmaceutical sensory perception | **SUPPORTED (narrowly)** | TAS2R38→PROP/PTC (replicated, incl. GWAS P=1.6e-104); TAS2R38→chloramphenicol and TAS2R9→ofloxacin bitterness (single cohort, n=143); clindamycin null (2026, n=67) bounds the claim to cognate-ligand drugs |
| 2 | A shared chemosensory genetic architecture extends across foods and medicines | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** | Same receptor system responds to food and drug ligands (BitterDB: 76 drug–receptor edges across 23 receptors; quinine, PROP straddle food/drug categories); but genotype→drug outcome replication is thin (1–2 cohorts) and one direct test was null |
| 3 | Ancient chemosensory variation structures modern human responses to novel chemical environments | **PLAUSIBLE, NOT DEMONSTRATED** | Evolutionary substrate is real (balancing selection at TAS2R38; 151 coding haplotypes; relaxed constraint). "Structures responses to medicines" currently rests on antibiotics + PROP; generalization unproven |
| 4 | The genotype–medication interface is environmentally plastic | **NOT DEMONSTRATED** | Adult sensory plasticity exists for salt preference/thresholds and sweet intensity, but bitter-specific hedonic plasticity is weak/null and no study links training → medication-relevant ΔS1 |
| 5 | Sensory conditioning can modify medication-related behavior despite genotype | **NOT DEMONSTRATED** | No direct interventional evidence exists; would be a novel contribution, not a claim to assert |

## Journal positioning (realistic, not prestige-selected)

- **Nature / Nature Genetics / Nature Medicine:** requires Level 3+ with
  validated population evidence and ideally the intervention trial — not
  achievable from dry evidence alone.
- **Nature Communications / Science Advances / PNAS:** plausible only if a
  powered ToMMo-type validation reproduces β3 genotype×drug-property
  effects with negative controls.
- **Am J Hum Genet:** needs genetic architecture novelty (GWAS-level);
  current dry package does not provide new association evidence.
- **Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics / Pharmacogenomics Journal:**
  realistic home for a validated sensory-pharmacogenomics result.
- **Chemical Senses:** natural home for the mechanistic/sensory core; the
  dry synthesis + locked prediction framework itself is publishable here
  or as a protocol.

The project is scientifically worthwhile even if Nature is unrealistic —
the strongest realistic contribution is a *pre-registered sensory
pharmacogenomic hypothesis with falsifiable predictions*, i.e. Levels 1–2.
