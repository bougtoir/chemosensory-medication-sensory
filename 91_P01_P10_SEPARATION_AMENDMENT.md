# 91 — P01/P10 confirmatory-separation amendment

Status: **FINAL PRE-OUTCOME AMENDMENT** (class 3 analytic, permitted —
made before any genotype–medication association was inspected).

## Original ambiguity
Earlier freeze files suggested a sparse P01 could be "collapsed" into the
P10 thiourea-class analysis:
- `74` rule 4: "P01 collapses into the P10 thiourea pool" → **revised**.
- `72` P01 rationale: "merge with P02 class if sparse" → **revised**.
- `72` P10: described as "rescue logic" → **reworded**.

## Revised rule (frozen)
1. **P01 remains its original drug-specific prediction** (TAS2R38 →
   propylthiouracil exposure), nothing else.
2. If P01 fails the pre-specified observability or power floor
   (`72`, `73`): P01 status = **PRE-SPECIFIED BUT UNTESTABLE**.
3. P01 must NOT be rescued, replaced, merged, redefined, or declared
   supported on the basis of P10 or any other prediction.
4. **P10 remains an independently frozen secondary confirmatory
   prediction** (TAS2R4 → thiourea-class exposure in AVI carriers). It is
   analyzed per its own specification regardless of whether P01 is
   testable.
5. A successful P10 result is NOT replication or confirmation of P01.
6. A failed P01 due solely to inadequate N is NOT a biological refutation.

## Scientific rationale
Drug-specific and class-level predictions have different estimands and
different power; merging them post-sparsity would convert an informative
absence into an apparent substitution and break the pre-specified
denominator (`13_v2_FINAL`). Independence preserves both the biological
claim (P01) and the feasibility claim (P10).

## Impact on confirmatory status
- Denominator fixed: 12 directional predictions; each is evaluated or
  declared UNTESTABLE on its own minimum-data requirement (`62`, `72`).
- No prediction's status may depend on another prediction's result.
- Authorization matrix (`87`) updated: class-level pooling of an
  underpowered drug-level prediction is now explicitly FORBIDDEN.
